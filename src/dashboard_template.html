RuralReach

Rural Service Accessibility Intelligence System · 640 districts · Census 2011 base
⬇ Master dataset (CSV) GitHub repository

How to read this

The RuralReach Accessibility Index (0–100)

Higher = better access. Each indicator is log-scaled where skewed and winsorised between the 5th and 95th percentile, averaged inside a pillar, then weighted:

30%Education
30%Transport
20%Health*
20%Livelihood

Rankings are robust: equal-weight rank correlation 0.99, random weights median 0.90.

Priority groups

  • Critical gap – farm-dependent (≥ median share of farm workers) and bottom-quartile RAI.
  • High priority – rural with medium access, or non-rural with low access.
  • Monitor / Well served – the rest.

Spatial clusters (Local Moran's I) show whether a low-access district sits in a contiguous cold spot.

Read with care

  • *Health is state-level (PHC doctor staffing 2017) – every district in a state shares that value.
  • Census 2011 attributes on 2011 districts; post-2011 splits were folded back into their parent.
  • Rurality is proxied by farm-worker share (no urban/rural split in the data).
  • No school-facility, internet or district PHC data were supplied, so no digital or facility pillar.
  • Scores are associations for prioritisation, not causal estimates.
